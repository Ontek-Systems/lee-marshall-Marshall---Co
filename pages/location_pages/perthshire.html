Services in Perthshire

What We Do Here

Perthshire properties do not follow a single template. There are Georgian townhouses in Crieff, Victorian stone villas in Auchterarder, converted farm buildings in Strathearn, and newer detached homes spread across the market towns. Lee has worked across all of it and understands what each building type demands.

House Extensions

Extending a stone-built Perthshire home takes care. The new work needs to read alongside the original fabric, not fight it. Lee sources matching materials where possible, plans the structural side thoroughly, and builds extensions that sit as though they have always been there.

Garage Conversions

A lot of Perthshire homes have an integral or attached garage sitting underused. Turning it into a home office, utility room or ground-floor bedroom adds real living space without needing planning permission in most cases. Lee assesses the structure, insulates it properly and finishes it to the same standard as the rest of the house.

Kitchen Renovations
