Privacy Policy

A plain-English explanation of how we handle your information. Short version: we only use it to get back to you.

Last updated: June 2026

Marshall & Co. Bespoke Home Improvements takes your privacy seriously. This page explains what information we collect when you contact us, how we use it, and how long we hold it. There are no hidden surprises.

Who We Are

Marshall & Co. Bespoke Home Improvements is a sole trader business operated by Lee Marshall, based at 2 Talisker Place, Perth, PH1 3GW, Scotland. You can reach us at info@marshallandco.uk or on 07940 568877.

What Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to give us. This typically happens when you:

  • Fill in a contact or quote request form on this website
  • Send us an email directly
  • Call us or leave a voicemail
  • Message us on Facebook

The information you provide may include your name, phone number, email address, and details about your property or project. We do not collect any payment information through this website.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information for one purpose: to respond to your enquiry and discuss your project with you. Specifically, that means:

  • Contacting you to arrange a site visit or consultation
  • Sending you a quote or estimate based on your requirements
  • Following up on an ongoing project

We do not send marketing emails. We do not pass your details to third parties. We do not use your information for any purpose beyond managing your enquiry and, if applicable, delivering your project.

How Long We Keep Your Information

If your enquiry leads to a project, we will keep your contact details and project records for as long as is reasonably necessary for warranty, insurance, and legal purposes. If your enquiry does not proceed, we do not retain your details beyond the period needed to close the conversation.

Your Rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Ask what information we hold about you
  • Request that we correct any inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your information
  • Object to us processing your information

To exercise any of these rights, simply contact us directly at info@marshallandco.uk. We will respond promptly.

Cookies and Analytics

This website does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics platforms. No information about your browsing behaviour is collected or shared.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever need to update this policy, the revised version will appear on this page with a new date at the top. We do not anticipate any significant changes, because our approach to your data is not going to change: we collect only what we need and we use it only to help you.

Questions

If you have any questions about how we handle your information, get in touch directly. We are a small team and we will give you a straight answer.
