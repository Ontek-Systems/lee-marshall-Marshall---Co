01 / What We Collect

The Information You Send Us

When you fill in our contact form, we collect only the details you choose to type into it. That is normally:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • Anything you tell us about the work you are planning

We do not ask for payment details through this website, and we do not collect anything beyond what you send us.

02 / Why We Collect It

To Talk To You About The Job

Your details are used for one purpose only: getting back in touch about your enquiry. That means replying to your message, arranging a visit to see the property, pricing the work, and keeping you updated while the project runs.

We do not send marketing emails, we do not run newsletters, and we will not contact you about anything unrelated to the work you asked about.

03 / Who Sees It

Nobody Outside The Business

Your information stays with Marshall & Co. We never sell it, rent it, or share it with third parties for marketing. The only exceptions are the ordinary tools we use to run the business, such as our email provider and the form service that delivers your enquiry to us, and anyone we are legally required to disclose information to.

04 / How Long We Keep It

Only As Long As It Is Useful

Enquiries are kept while we are in conversation with you and for a reasonable period afterwards, so we have a record of quotes given and work carried out. Where a job goes ahead, we keep the relevant records for as long as we need them for warranty, insurance and tax purposes. Once details are no longer needed, they are deleted.

05 / Your Rights

Ask Us To Remove It Any Time

Under UK data protection law you can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Send a message to info@marshallandco.uk or call 07940 568877 and we will sort it out. If you are ever unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can raise it with the Information Commissioner's Office.

06 / Cookies

Nothing Following You Around

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies to build a profile of you. Any basic data recorded by our hosting provider is used purely to keep the site secure and running properly.
